# 📊 LinkedIn Job Posting Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

The **LinkedIn Job Posting Analysis** project focuses on exploring job market trends using data analytics. The project analyzes job postings to understand in-demand job titles, hiring companies, job locations, experience levels, and employment types.

Using Python, SQL, and Power BI, this project demonstrates a data analytics workflow, from data cleaning and exploratory data analysis to querying and visualization.

**Dataset Size:** 999 job postings

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze job titles and identify frequently advertised positions.
* Explore companies posting job opportunities.
* Understand job location distributions.
* Examine job levels and experience requirements.
* Analyze employment types and job categories.
* Identify patterns in job postings to better understand the job market.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Collection

Prepared a dataset containing 999 LinkedIn job postings for analysis.

### 2. Data Cleaning Using Python

* Inspected the dataset structure and data types.
* Checked for missing values and inconsistencies.
* Prepared the relevant columns for analysis.
* Organized the dataset for further processing.

### 3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Explored job posting data to understand:

* Frequently listed job titles.
* Companies advertising job opportunities.
* Job locations and geographical patterns.
* Job levels and employment types.

### 4. SQL Analysis

Used SQL queries to explore the dataset and analyze job postings based on relevant attributes, including job titles, companies, locations, and job levels.

### 5. Power BI Visualization

Used Power BI to present job market information through visualizations and make the findings easier to understand.

## 📈 Key Areas of Analysis

* **Job Title Analysis:** Explore the distribution of advertised positions.
* **Company Analysis:** Identify companies with frequent job postings in the dataset.
* **Location Analysis:** Examine the locations associated with job opportunities.
* **Job Level Analysis:** Understand the distribution of different job levels.
* **Employment Type Analysis:** Explore the types of employment advertised.
* **Job Market Trends:** Identify patterns within the available job postings.
